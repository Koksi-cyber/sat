BitSats: Buy & Sell Through BaseScan  
One-page explorer guide for using the router contract

A. Buy tokens with ETH  
1. Open the router contract on BaseScan and connect your wallet: Contract -> Write Contract -> Connect to Web3.  
2. Find the buy function. In the Value field, enter the ETH amount to spend, e.g. 0.001 ETH.  
3. Enter the PoolKey exactly as required by the form. Use native ETH as currency0: 0x0000000000000000000000000000000000000000 . 
4. Use your wallet address as recipient.  
5. Click Write, approve the wallet prompt, then wait for confirmation.  
B. Approve the router before selling  
1. Open the token contract on BaseScan: Contract -> Write Contract -> Connect to Web3.  
2. Call approve(spender, amount).  
approve field  Enter this  
spender  0x97137d335b29743E1b9434f8Cc594D50572dE5a7  
amount  115792089237316195423570985008687907853269984665640564039457584007913129639935  
This amount is max uint256. It gives the router a maximum allowance, so users do not need to approve before every sell. Only approve contracts you 
trust.  
C. Sell tokens for ETH  
1. Open the router contract again: Contract -> Write Contract -> Connect to Web3.  
2. Find the sell function.  
3. Enter the same PoolKey used for buying. Enter your wallet as recipient.  
4. Enter the token amount in 18 -decimal units. Example: 1 token = 1000000000000000000.  
5. Click Write and confirm in your wallet.  
PoolKey reminder  
currency0  0x0000000000000000000000000000000000000000  
currency1  Token contract address above  
fee 3000 
tickSpacing  60 
hooks  Hook contract address used when the pool was initialized  
tuple format  (currency0,currency1,3000,60,hooks)  
Safety notes:  
• Use the correct network and explorer. Buy transactions must include ETH in the Value field.  
• If BaseScan says the transaction may revert, check the PoolKey, wallet balance, approval.
